The idea for the Energy Consumption Web Application stemmed from the growing global emphasis on sustainable energy and the need for accessible tools to analyze energy consumption patterns. With the rise in renewable energy adoption, such as solar, wind, and biofuels, there is a demand for platforms that allow stakeholders—policymakers, researchers, and the public—to visualize and interpret energy-related data effectively.
Case Study: Global Energy Trends
According to the International Energy Agency (IEA), global renewable energy capacity grew by 10.3% in 2023, with solar and wind energy leading the transition. However, disparities in energy consumption and economic factors (e.g., GDP) across countries highlight the need for data-driven insights. For instance, countries with high GDP often have higher energy consumption but vary in their reliance on renewables. A web-based tool that displays such data interactively can aid in understanding these trends and informing policy decisions
